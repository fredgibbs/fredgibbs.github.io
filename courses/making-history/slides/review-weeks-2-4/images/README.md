## Notes

**Historic images only, and only ones students have seen before.** This review
is meant to reinforce images from earlier sessions, not add new ones, and to
keep students in the period. An earlier draft used four modern images: a 2015
Gettysburg staff ride, a Moore's law chart, the Kew reading room and NASA's
earth at night. The instructor had them removed. Don't add modern images back.

**The Ranke preface page is a crop.** The regenerate command is in
`../../scientific-history/images/README.md`.
