# Slide Images — The History of Science (13.1)

Twenty-seven image files, all used by `../index.md` (sixth revision, 2026-11-10).
Most are public domain or CC0; the Ifá tray (CC BY-SA 4.0), the Jaipur
observatory photograph (CC BY 4.0) and the Wellcome images, including the Bombay
plague photograph, (CC BY 4.0) are credited in their captions or figcaptions.
The deck shows things and places; it carries no portrait of a historian.
Credits are carried in the `<figcaption>`s and should not be stripped.
Retrieved 2026-11-06, 2026-11-07, 2026-11-09 and 2026-11-10. The folder totals about 7.8 MB.

**Split 2026-11-11.** The Science and Medicine deck (13.1) was split into `history-of-science` and `history-of-medicine`; each folder now holds only the images its own deck uses, and the credits table below lists only those. The counts, totals and notes that follow were written for the combined deck, so some refer to images now in the other folder.

## Notes

**Recompressed 2026-11-07** to stay near 10 MB: Imperato, Board, the Gazetier
and Cruikshank at q72, and Ulrich resized to 1300px. In the third revision, to
make room for six new images: the explainer images (Tycho, the Gazetier, the
Strasbourg clock, the Ifá tray, Cruikshank, Edenton, Kneller's Newton) were
resized to 850–900px tall, about twice their displayed size, and Snow, the
Harvard computers, Shapin and Brouillet were resaved at q72–74. Some row
dimensions above predate this.

**Landscape images are cropped for explainers.** An explainer's image is
capped at 470×380, so a landscape picture squeezes the text column and pushes
the slide past 644px. The six landscape images in part three's explainers are
cropped toward portrait around the scene that matters.

**Explainer credits are short.** The explainer's figure is as narrow as its
image, so a full caption wraps to three lines and pushes the slide past the
checker's 644px line. The credits on all eighteen explainers (five in part one,
five in part two, eight in part three) are cut to a line, and each image is
identified in the `.quote` cite or the `.detail` beside it instead. Full identifications are in the table above.

**Retired 2026-11-10,** with the slides that used them (also Newton's monument, Boehm's Darwin, the Yoruba tray, the Solvay photograph, the Voltaire frontispiece, the *Sidereus Nuncius* title page and Snow's cholera map), and moved to the session
scratchpad (Rembrandt's *Anatomy Lesson* left when the deck began at the Solvay

heroic image of epidemiology; the caption notes Snow's own observation that
