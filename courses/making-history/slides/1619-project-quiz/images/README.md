## Notes

- **Captions.** The Dunmore, Woodard and Du Bois captions are the sister decks'
  captions word for word. The 1862 report's caption is rewritten to say what
  the meeting was, since this deck has no slide on the two printings. The
  Declaration's caption quotes Friedersdorf instead of the essay, because here
  it sits before the founding-date question.
- **The bleed openers** follow the review-deck rule (`SLIDE-STYLE.md`, "A review
  deck"): each part opens on a full-bleed image instead of a portrait. Their
  `.credit` lines carry the credit; Gettysburg's adds no caption, so Maza's
  remark about the excluded Black veterans (in the inventing-nations caption)
  is left for the speaker.
- **Resolution.** `gettysburg-reunion-1913.jpg` is 987×759, about 1.08× when
  shown contained on the 960×700 slide: sharp at deck size, soft on a 1080p
  projector. It is the inventing-nations deck's copy; a larger LOC scan
  (ppmsca.58372) was not fetched. `deck-check.sh` does not measure background
  images, so it does not warn about this one.
