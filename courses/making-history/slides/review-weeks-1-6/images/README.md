# Slide Images — What Makes History Happen? (review of Weeks 1–6)

Four images used by `../index.md`, **all copied from the decks this one
reviews**, all public domain or CC0. They are byte-for-byte copies of the files
in the decks named below; if one is re-cropped or replaced there, copy it here
again. If you change a filename, update the matching `<img src="…">` or
`data-background-image` in `../index.md`.

Nothing here is new to the students. That is the point: each image is one they
have already sat with, so a review can put it up cold and ask the room what it
was for. Don't swap one for a fresh picture of the same subject — a picture
they have never seen is a new argument to introduce, not a cue to recall.

## Notes

All four are full-bleed, one at the head of each writer's slide in Weeks 5–6,
and each is the image that week was built on: the mill nobody voted for, the
hillside that outlasts the battle, the cropper in a dress, the room with no
women in it. They replace the four portraits this deck used to open those
weeks with — see `../ISSUES.md`.
