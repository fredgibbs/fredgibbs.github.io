# Issues — review-weeks-1-6

**Resolved, deliberate deviation.** `schedule.md`'s own 7.2 discussion prompts
(as of 2026-09-24) name "Davis" twice — once beside Thompson as a purpose
("Davis wants to understand how people made sense of their world") and once
beside Braudel on scale ("Davis explains identity through one impersonation
case"). No reading by Natalie Zemon Davis is assigned anywhere in Weeks 1–7;
7.1 assigns only Ginzburg, *The Cheese and the Worms*. This looks like a
leftover from an earlier draft of the syllabus where Davis (likely *The
Return of Martin Guerre*) was paired with or swapped for Ginzburg on
microhistory.

Discussion slides 1 and 4 in this deck use Ginzburg instead of Davis
throughout, since Ginzburg is the only microhistorian students actually read.
Discussion slide 2 drops the Davis clause rather than force a substitute
purpose onto him. `schedule.md` itself was left unchanged — worth a look
next time it's revised, but not this deck's call to make.

**Provenance, not a defect.** Every direct quotation in this deck (Carr,
Marx/Engels, Braudel, Thompson, Scott) is reused verbatim from its own
standalone deck (`carr-historian-and-facts`, `marx-structural-history`,
`annales-longue-duree`, `history-from-below`, `gender`), where it was already
checked against the source. This deck did not re-verify against the primary
PDFs; it trusted the existing decks' citations. If one of those decks is ever
corrected, check back here.

**Images** are referenced by relative path into the four source decks'
`images/` folders rather than copied — see their own `images/README.md` for
credits. No new images were added here.
