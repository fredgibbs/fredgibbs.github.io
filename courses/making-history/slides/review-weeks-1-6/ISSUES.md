**The throughline is scale, not "what is history for," as of 2026-10-01.**
Right after the linear-by-week pass below, Fred pointed out that Week 7's own
title in `schedule.md` is "Scales of History," and tonight's reading
refraction is explicitly about "scale and the cost of zooming in or out" —
purpose is downstream of that question, not the top-level one. So every
closing `.question` line (12 of them: Carr, the six Weeks 2–4 pairs, Marx,
Braudel, Thompson, Scott, Ginzburg) was rewritten again, this time asking what
scale that pair or writer operates at — a single war's cause, one report, a
civilization's worth of exemplary acts, the whole species, the single
document, lived relationships — rather than what they think history is for.
The three brief week-recaps (Week 2, 3, 4) were rewritten the same way. The
title slide changed from "What Makes History Happen?" to "Every Historian
Picks a Scale," and the front-matter `title:` to match.
