## Deliberate choices

- **"Largely alone" is Serwer's quotation.** His article gives it as
  Hannah-Jones's phrase; the PDF's wording is "For the most part, black
  Americans fought back alone" (p. 24). The verdicts slide quotes the essay's
  sentence, then Hannah-Jones's defense of it to Serwer, then Serwer's
  "largely alone." A later review should not "correct" Serwer's quotation to the
  PDF's; the discrepancy suggests the online text was worded differently from
  the print.
- **The "DNA" line** ("Anti-black racism runs in the very DNA of this country")
  is p. 21; the "case against" card gives the page and quotes the phrase.
- **Quotations keep the source's lowercase "black."** The deck's own voice says
  "Black Americans." Serwer's and Hannah-Jones's 2019 texts use lowercase.
- **Single-appearance names on the "case for" slide** (Blight, Painter) and
  Pyle's name in his caption stay: each is the speaker or maker of the thing
  quoted or shown, and the cards would lose their evidence without them.
- **No "compare" blocks and no course cross-references.** The discussion post
  asks students to find the connections (Ranke, Carr, Trouillot, Anderson, the
  Enlightenment idea of progress) themselves, so the deck asks the question
  ("where evidence stops," the objectivity slide) and does not answer it. Add
  `.reveal-block.compare` blocks if that is not wanted.
- **Not covered: the Week 12.2 Trouillot question about the project being banned
  from state curricula.** No reading in hand speaks to it. Serwer's curriculum
  material is only the SPLC finding and Silverstein's remark that the materials
  supplement rather than replace traditional curricula.
- **The deck does not mention the Pulitzer Center curriculum, the later book, or
  the 2020–21 curriculum fights**, all of which postdate or sit outside the
  three readings.
- **The schedule links the deck under 12.2** (added 2026-11-02, with the
  Friedersdorf bullet). It is not linked under 12.1; add the same line there if
  part one should be available on Tuesday. The schedule gives the essay's date
  as August 19, 2019; the PDF's masthead says August 18 (the Magazine's issue
  date; the web version ran August 14). The deck uses August 18.
- **The Pyle image is 908×938** and clears the 1.5× check only as a landscape
  figure (see `images/README.md`).
