## Notes

- **The Luddite print is cropped because its engraved title is the answer.**
  The bottom margin reads "THE LEADER OF THE LUDDITES". The crop, and the
  command that regenerates it, are documented in
  `../../review-jeopardy/images/README.md`.

- **Every picture was checked for text that gives the answer away.** The
  power-loom plate is the deck's crop, without its engraved title. The Croesus
  vase carries his name in Greek letters, which almost no one in the room will
  read. The paschal table's column heads read *PASCHAE*, which hands the
  Easter question to a student reading Latin — left in on purpose.

- **The paschal table is CC BY-NC 4.0** (e-codices). The credit is shown with
  the answer; the use here is non-commercial teaching, as in the Week 3 deck it
  comes from.
