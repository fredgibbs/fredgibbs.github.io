## Board's Jenner recaptioned, 2026-11-12

The author asked whether the painting "really show[s] the doctor as hero
aesthetic." It does not: even light, Jenner seated level with the boy, the
woman as large as he is. It is kept as the reassuring version of the old story
(gentle, willing, no risk shown), which the Gillray cartoon later answers with
what patients feared. The caption no longer calls it "the physicians' version."
Outside fact in the new caption: James Phipps was healthy when Jenner
inoculated him with cowpox.
